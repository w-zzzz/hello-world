By 2026 there are roughly a dozen seriously frontier-class large language
models, from five or six labs, with a small open-weights group close behind.
This module is a tour of the 2025-26 landscape: who ships what, what each
optimizes for, and where the open/closed divide actually sits.

This is a snapshot. It will be wrong in eighteen months. It is included
because the *axes* of differentiation — reasoning, multimodality, context
length, openness, cost — are durable even as the specific products move.

## The 2025-26 frontier, by lab

### Anthropic

Claude 3.5 Sonnet (June 2024) → Claude 3.5 Sonnet New (October 2024,
introduced Computer Use) → Claude 3.7 Sonnet (February 2025, introduced
extended thinking and Claude Code) → Claude 4 Sonnet/Opus (May 2025).
Anthropic's positioning is *helpful, honest, harmless* with strong agentic
coding and a coherent safety story (RSP). Computer Use is a defining product.

### OpenAI

GPT-4o (May 2024, omni-modal) → o1 (Sep 2024, dedicated reasoning) →
o3 / o3-mini (Jan 2025) → GPT-4.1 (April 2025, long context) → GPT-5
(Aug 2025, unified reasoning + general). The OpenAI portfolio splits
between *general* models (GPT-N) and *reasoning* models (o-series); GPT-5
unifies the two with a user-controllable reasoning budget. Operator and
the Codex agent are the products built on top.

### Google DeepMind

Gemini 1.5 Pro (Feb 2024, 1M context) → Gemini 2.0 Flash/Pro (Dec 2024) →
Gemini 2.5 Pro (March 2025, dedicated thinking) → Gemini 3 (late 2025).
DeepMind's bets: long context, native multimodality (audio, video, images
ingested natively), tight Google integration (Workspace, Search, Project
Mariner, Project Astra). Gemini Robotics is the bet on embodied.

### DeepSeek

DeepSeek-V3 (Dec 2024) → DeepSeek-R1 (Jan 2025) → DeepSeek-V3.1 / R1.5
(2025). The story is *frontier capability at fractional cost*: a 671B MoE
with 37B active, FP8 training, MLA, custom load balancing. DeepSeek-R1's
release on permissive license shifted the open-weights frontier overnight.

### Meta

Llama 3.1 (July 2024, 405B dense) → Llama 3.3 (Dec 2024) → Llama 4 herd
(April 2025: Scout, Maverick, Behemoth). Llama 4 went MoE and ultra-long
context (10M tokens for Scout). Meta's distinguishing bet remains *open
weights at the frontier*; whether the latest releases keep pace with closed
competitors is the open question.

### Mistral

Mistral Large 2 (July 2024) → Mistral Small 3 (Jan 2025) → Magistral
(June 2025, reasoning model). Smaller European lab, Apache-2 weights for
many releases, strong on European languages.

### Qwen (Alibaba)

Qwen2.5 (Sep 2024) → Qwen3 (April 2025). The Qwen3 family covers 0.6B
through 235B-MoE on permissive licenses; Qwen3-Coder and Qwen3-VL hold
state-of-the-art positions on several open-model leaderboards.

## Axes of differentiation

Treat the leaderboard as a noisy aggregate. The *axes* are more useful:

| Axis | Strong contenders (mid-2026) |
|---|---|
| Frontier reasoning | GPT-5, Claude 4 Opus, Gemini 3, DeepSeek-R1.5 |
| Long context (≥1M tokens) | Gemini 2.5/3, GPT-4.1, Llama 4 Scout, Claude 4 |
| Agentic coding | Claude 4, GPT-5, DeepSeek-V3.1 |
| Multimodal (image+audio+video native) | Gemini 2.5/3, GPT-4o/5 |
| Computer use / tool use | Claude 4, GPT-5 (Operator), Gemini Mariner |
| Cheapest serious model | DeepSeek-V3.1, Qwen3 |
| Openness (commercial-friendly weights) | Llama 4, DeepSeek, Qwen3, Mistral |
| On-device | Apple Intelligence (Apple Foundation), Phi-4, Gemma 3, Llama 3.2 1B/3B |

A useful exercise: pick a real workload (RAG over your docs, coding agent,
voice product) and rank the models on the axes that matter, not the
overall MMLU number.

## What changed in 2025

A few structural shifts in the last 12-18 months matter for everyone:

1. **Reasoning effort as a dial.** o1, R1, Claude 3.7, Gemini 2.5 Thinking,
   GPT-5 all expose user-controllable test-time compute. The product
   metaphor of "fast vs careful" is now standard.
2. **MoE and KV-cache compression won.** Frontier dense scaling above
   ~400B is rare; MoE (DeepSeek, Mixtral, Llama 4, Gemini 1.5+) plus
   MLA / GQA dominate.
3. **Costs collapsed.** Token costs for top-tier models dropped by 5-50x
   from late 2023 to mid-2026. DeepSeek-V3 priced API tokens at fractions
   of a cent, dragging closed-model prices down.
4. **Native multimodality became table stakes.** Audio-in / audio-out,
   image, video — Gemini-style native multimodality is the default
   architecture for new models.
5. **Open-weights gap stabilized at ~6 months.** Open frontier models
   match the closed frontier from half a year ago. The gap stopped
   narrowing in 2025; it also did not widen.

## Reading the leaderboards

Three useful aggregators in 2026:

- **Artificial Analysis** for capability + cost + speed dashboards across
  most production APIs.
- **LMArena (formerly LMSYS Chatbot Arena)** for blind human preference;
  the leaderboard is noisy but directionally useful.
- **METR / AISI evaluations** for safety-relevant capabilities (autonomy,
  cyber, bio uplift). The most rigorous public numbers, when available.

Be skeptical of any single benchmark. A model can top MMLU and still be
unusable for your task. Build your own evals.

```python
# A pragmatic 'pick a model' template.
WORKLOAD = "agentic-coding"  # or "rag", "voice", "translation", ...
CONSTRAINTS = {
    "max_p50_latency_ms": 5000,
    "open_weights_required": False,
    "max_cost_per_1m_output_tokens_usd": 30,
    "min_context_tokens": 200_000,
}
# Score each candidate model on the axes that matter for WORKLOAD,
# filtered by CONSTRAINTS. Re-run quarterly. The 'best' answer changes.
```
